Audio Bubble

Privacy Policy

Updated September 26, 2026

Audio Bubble doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Nothing you say or do in the app reaches the developer or anyone else you haven't chosen to hear you.

Your voice

While you're in a bubble, the app sends your voice from your microphone directly to the phones of the people in your bubble, over Wi-Fi between your phones. It isn't sent anywhere else, and it's never recorded or stored. When you're not in a bubble, or you mute, no audio from your microphone is sent.

Your name, color and bubble

The name, color and Memoji or emoji you choose are saved only on your phone. So that people nearby can see and invite you, your phone shares them with other phones nearby that have Audio Bubble open. You can change them at any time in the app.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to send your voice to the people in your bubble.
  • Local Network: to find people nearby and send your voice directly to them, with or without a Wi-Fi network.

Children

Audio Bubble collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions are welcome on the Support page.
