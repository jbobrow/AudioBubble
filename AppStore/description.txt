Hear the people you're with, clearly and instantly, even in loud places.

At a concert, a busy bar, a crowded street or a noisy workshop, Audio Bubble puts the people you're with right in your ears. Put in your headphones, tap the friends you want, and once they join, you're in a bubble together. You hear everyone in the bubble, and never yourself.

NO NETWORK NEEDED
Audio Bubble works phone to phone. Keep Wi-Fi on, but you don't need to join a network, and your bubble actually sounds best without one. If you're joined to a Wi-Fi network, the app tells you the one-tap way to leave it.

INSTANT, NOT A PHONE CALL
There's no dialing, no links and no accounts. People nearby with Audio Bubble float on your screen. Tap someone to invite them; they tap Join. Your voice goes straight to their phone as uncompressed, full-quality audio, with no codec delay, so conversation feels natural.

BUILT FOR NOISE
Apple's voice processing cancels echo and reduces background noise, and you can switch on Voice Isolation right from your bubble. When friends stand close together, their mics pick up your voice too; Audio Bubble finds your voice in their audio and turns it down, so you don't hear yourself echoing back.

MADE FOR THE PEOPLE YOU'RE WITH
• Tap to invite; there's no host, so anyone can leave without ending the bubble
• See everyone in one circle, glowing as they talk
• Mute or leave with one tap
• Keeps going with your screen locked
• Choose your name, a color, and a Memoji or any emoji for your bubble

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your voice goes only to the people in your bubble, directly between your phones. There are no accounts and no servers, and nothing is recorded.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Headphones: AirPods or any wired or Bluetooth headphones. They're required, so only your bubble hears the audio. Bluetooth headphones add some delay of their own.
• Everyone in the bubble needs Audio Bubble on their own iPhone, nearby, with Wi-Fi turned on.
